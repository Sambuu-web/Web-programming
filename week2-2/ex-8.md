      <section>
        <h2>Background</h2>
        <p>Technology has always played an important role in education.
           Students once depended mainly on textbooks and classroom lectures,
           but today they can access thousands of online resources within
           seconds. The development of <strong>artificial intelligence</strong>
           has introduced another major change.</p>

        <p>AI systems can help students understand difficult topics, organize
           information, practice languages, and find new ways to solve
           problems. Instead of replacing traditional learning, AI can be
           used as a tool to support students when it is used responsibly.</p>
      </section>

      <section>
        <h2>Analysis</h2>
        <p>One of the biggest advantages of AI in education is
           <mark>personalized learning</mark>. Students can ask questions
           at their own pace and receive explanations that match their level.
           This can be especially useful when studying programming,
           mathematics, languages, and other technical subjects.</p>

        <p>However, students should not depend completely on AI.
           Understanding the answer and developing independent
           <strong>critical thinking skills</strong> are still essential.
           AI should be treated as a learning assistant rather than a
           replacement for personal effort.</p>
      </section>

      <section>
        <h2>Future of Education</h2>
        <p>As artificial intelligence continues to develop, its role in
           education will likely become even larger. Universities may use
           intelligent systems to create personalized study plans,
           provide instant feedback, and help students practice new skills.</p>

        <p>For students studying technology today, learning how to use AI
           responsibly can become an important part of their future careers.
           The combination of human creativity and modern technology can
           create new opportunities in many different fields.</p>
